## 🔬 About us

We are a **chemometrics and machine-learning** research group at the Department of Chemistry, University of Turin. We develop and rigorously
validate multivariate and ML models that turn high-dimensional instrumental data into
chemically meaningful, interpretable answers.

**Areas of interest**

- 🔍 **Forensic chemistry** — analytical evidence interpretation and modelling
- 🩺 **Clinical chemistry** — disease classification and stratification from omics data
- 🍷 **Food chemistry** — flavour/aroma characterization, authentication, fraud detection

---

## 🧪 Instrumental platforms


- **UHPLC–HRMS** (high-resolution MS)
- **UHPLC–MS/MS** (targeted/quantitative MS)
- **GC×GC–TOF** (comprehensive two-dimensional GC)
- **Hyperspectral Imaging** [NIREOS HERA](https://nireos.com/hera-hyperspectral-cameras/) Fourier-transform cameras (VNIR)

## 🎓 School of Chemometrics & Machine Learning with Python

We run the **Scuola di Chemiometria e Machine Learning con Python**, a hands-on,
Python-based school held at the Department of Chemistry, University of Turin, within
the **CH4.0 "Dipartimento di Eccellenza 2023–2027"** project, jointly with our spin-off
**DataBloom**. Two editions per year. <!-- CHECK: page lists the 4th edition, 23–27 Feb 2026 -->

Topics span univariate statistics and hypothesis testing, unsupervised models (PCA,
cluster analysis), supervised classification (kNN, LDA/QDA, PLS-DA, RF, SIMCA), and
regression (OLS, MLR, PCR, PLS-R), worked entirely in Python (NumPy, pandas,
Matplotlib/Seaborn, Plotly, SciPy, scikit-learn) via Jupyter / Google Colab.
